# Guidance Office Verification Questionnaire

**Purpose:** Validate/refine empirical claims in the SecureCAT manuscript that require Guidance Office insights.
**Date Prepared:** June 14, 2026
**Target Respondents:** Guidance Counselors (2–3), Guidance Office Head
**Mode:** Semi-structured interview + Google Form follow-up
**Researcher Role:** Neutral researcher (no mention of "SecureCAT-v2", "V2", or prototype)

---

## Interview Protocol

### Opening (2 minutes)
> "Thank you for your time. I'm conducting research on the college admission testing process at ISPSC Tagudin Campus. Your office plays a central role in examination scoring and applicant guidance. The questions below are about current workflows — there are no right or wrong answers. Your responses will inform a descriptive developmental study; all data will be anonymized in any publication."

## Google Form Structure (for asynchronous follow-up)

### Form Title: ISPSC Guidance Office — Admission Workflow Validation

### Form Description:
This form validates workflow details discussed in our interview for a BSIT capstone study on college admission testing. Responses are anonymized. Estimated time: 10–15 minutes.

### Sections & Questions

**Section 1: Respondent Info**
- Q1: Your role in Guidance Office (Dropdown: Counselor / Office Head / Staff / Other)
- Q2: Years in this role (Short answer)

**Section 2: Scoring Process**
- Q3: How many admission cycles have you scored using the stencil method? (Short answer)
- Q4: Typical scoring duration for one cycle (Multiple choice: 1 day / 2 days / 3 days / 4+ days / Other)
- Q5: Number of staff involved in scoring (Short answer)
- Q6: Most common scoring errors you've encountered (Checkboxes: Misalignment / Double marks / Erasures / Tally errors / Illegible marks / None / Other)
- Q7: Do you maintain an error log? (Yes/No)
- Q8: If yes, approximate error rate per 100 sheets (Short answer)

**Section 3: Consultation Workflow**
- Q9: Average consultations per day during peak (Short answer)
- Q10: Average session length in minutes (Short answer)
- Q11: Top 3 applicant questions during consultation (Long answer)
- Q12: Data sources you reference during consultation (Checkboxes: Exam scores / High school grades / Program quotas / Career info / Program requirements / Other)
- Q12b: How do you check program slot availability? (Dropdown: Printed spreadsheet / Digital file / Verbal from Registrar / Memory / Other)
- Q13: Rate the mental effort during peak consultation (Linear scale: 1 = Very Low to 5 = Very High)

**Section 4: Applicant Travel & Visits**
- Q14: Top 5 municipalities of applicants (Long answer)
- Q15: Typical number of campus visits per applicant (Dropdown: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4+ / Varies)
- Q16: Purposes of visits (Checkboxes: Submit requirements / Check exam schedule / Attend exam / Claim results / Consultation / Other)
- Q17: Have you heard applicants mention travel time >2 hours? (Yes / No / Unsure)
- Q18: If yes, which areas? (Long answer)

**Section 5: Equipment & Readiness**
- Q19: Current scanner/camera for document processing (Short answer: model or "none")
- Q20: Are answer sheets pre-printed with QR codes? (Yes / No / Partially)
- Q21: If no QR codes, how is applicant ID marked on sheets? (Short answer)

**Section 6: Open Feedback**
- Q22: One thing you'd change about the current scoring process (Long answer)
- Q23: One thing you'd change about the consultation process (Long answer)
- Q24: Any concerns about digitizing these workflows? (Long answer)
