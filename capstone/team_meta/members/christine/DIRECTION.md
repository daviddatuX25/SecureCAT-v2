**Priority:** HIGH — Local foundation for the study

**What to Do:**

   - **Identify and describe:**
     - **Guidance Office processes:** How do they currently handle admission testing? Manual scheduling? Paper-based forms?

   - **Note specific pain points:** Where does the current process fail? Delayed results? Lost test papers? Lack of coordination between offices?

2. **Research Comparable Regional Institutions** (60 min):

     - "Ilocos Region university admission systems"

   - If regional studies are scarce, broaden to:
     - "Philippine university admission processes"
     - "guidance office information systems Philippines"
   - Target: 3-5 sources on similar institutions or regional ICT in education

3. **Write Local Context Paragraph** (90-120 min):
   - **10-15 sentences** describing ISPSC Tagudin's specific operational environment
   - **Structure:**
     - Sentences 1-3: ISPSC Tagudin context (location, student population, programs offered)
     - Sentences 4-7: Current manual processes in Guidance and Registrar offices (what they do now)
     - Sentences 8-10: Operational constraints (infrastructure, staffing, connectivity)
     - Sentences 11-15: Precedent from comparable institutions (what other Philippine SUCs are doing)
   - **Include minimum 5 in-text citations** (Author, Year) — can be mix of local/regional sources
   - **Be specific:** Name actual processes, constraints, and institutions
   - **Example sentence:** "At ISPSC Tagudin, the Guidance Office manually schedules admission tests using paper forms, while the Registrar separately records scores in spreadsheets, a process that results in delayed results and no audit trail (Author, 2024)."

4. **Compile Draft References** (30 min):

**Priority:** HIGH — Local literature foundation

   - Search Google Scholar for:

This fragmentation creates delays and errors that systems like SecureCAT — with role-based coordination and automated scoring — can address (Author C, 2025)."

   - **Create a master list** of all sources

2. **Format All References in APA 7** (90-120 min):
   - **Alphabetical order:** Sort by first author's surname (A-Z)
   - **APA 7 Format:**
     - **Journal Article:** Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Periodical, xx*(x), pp–pp. https://doi.org/xxxxx
     - **Book:** Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work*. Publisher. DOI/URL
     - **Website:** Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work*. Site Name. URL
   - **Hanging indent:** First line flush left, subsequent lines indented
   - **Italics:** Italicize journal titles, volume numbers, book titles
   - **Capitalization:** Sentence case for article titles, title case for journal/book titles

3. **Cross-Check Citations** (30-60 min):
   - **Verify every in-text citation has a reference entry:**
     - Scan all chapters for (Author, Year) citations
     - Check each citation against the master list
     - Flag missing references
   - **Verify every reference entry is cited in text:**
     - Go through master list alphabetically
     - Check each reference against in-text citations
     - Flag orphaned references (not cited)
   - **Check 2022-2026 compliance:**
     - Flag any sources outside the 2022-2026 range
     - These must be justified or replaced

4. **Create Final References List** (30 min):
   - Compile all verified references into a single alphabetized list
   - Save as `CC-01_References_List.md`
   - This will be inserted into the final manuscript
