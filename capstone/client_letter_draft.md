We, the undersigned Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) students, are currently working on our capstone project titled **"SecureCAT: A Role-Based College Admission Testing System for the Guidance and Registrar Offices at ISPSC Tagudin."** We have recently defended this title successfully as a requirement for our BSIT Capstone course.

The primary objective of SecureCAT is to design, develop, and implement a secure, role-based system that streamlines the college admission testing process, from application processing (Registrar) to exam scheduling, proctoring, score grading, and consultation release (Guidance).

In line with this, we respectfully request permission to conduct data gathering, workflow analysis, system evaluation, and eventual pilot testing within the Guidance and Registrar Offices of the Tagudin Campus. We assure you that all information collected during this study will be held with the utmost confidentiality and will be used strictly for academic purposes and system optimization.
