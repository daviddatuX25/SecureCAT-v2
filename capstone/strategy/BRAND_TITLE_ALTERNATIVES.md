## 🎭 Defense Strategy: How to Present the Change

### If Panel Asks: "Why change the name?"
> **Honest, professional response:**
> "During stakeholder validation, we received feedback that 'SecureCAT' could be misread informally. We proactively evaluated alternatives that better reflect the system's institutional purpose — security, guidance, and admission integrity — without linguistic ambiguity. The architecture, features, and evaluation remain unchanged; only the brand identifier is updated to align with professional naming conventions in educational technology."

### If Panel Asks: "Does this affect the study?"
> "No. The research objectives, methodology, instruments (SUS+TAM), and developmental scope are identical. The name change is a presentation-layer decision made during the Operations phase (AIDLC) based on user-facing feedback — exactly the kind of iterative refinement AIDLC encourages."
